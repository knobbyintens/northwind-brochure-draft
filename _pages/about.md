# About Northwind Fieldwork

Northwind Fieldwork Limited was founded in 2013 and is headquartered in Porto,
Portugal. We work across consumer health, financial services and mobility,
helping organisations turn messy human evidence into confident product and
brand decisions.

## Leadership

**Inês Cardoso, Managing Partner.** Inês leads the studio from Porto, focusing
on brand strategy and social impact.
